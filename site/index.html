Adaptive nicotine tapering

Your flavour never changes. Only the nicotine wanes.

Every quit product tapers how often you vape. Wane tapers how much nicotine is in each puff, from your measured behaviour, so you never have to decide when to step down. Scroll to follow one taper from a 40 % nicotine share to zero.

The problem

The ladder does not lose people to relapse. It loses them to stalling.

A fixed ladder cuts a set percentage per step and asks you to switch liquid. Most people manage the chemistry. What they skip is the decision, and a step never applied is a taper that has quietly stopped.

1largest effect in the simulation: taking the decision away from the user, with automatic delivery
The signal

Seven days of puffs say who is about to stall, a week ahead.

The device counts and times every puff with a pressure sensor. Night puffs and time to first puff carry most of the signal. Puffs per hour and puff length fill in the rest.

7 dwindow of measured behaviour behind every decision
The engine

Estimate craving. Estimate risk of relapse. Then cut, half cut, or hold.

Each period the app turns the last week into features, scores next week's relapse risk and current craving, and picks one of three moves. It can only lower or hold, never raise. The device measures. The app decides.

3moves: cut, half cut, hold. The per-person rate is learned, not set.
The device

Two chambers. One ring. No numbers on the hardware.

A flavour chamber you fill with any 0 mg liquid, and a small nicotine chamber from a standard 20 mg/ml shot. Each has its own coil. The app lowers the nicotine coil's share of the power week by week, so the dose falls while the flavour stays the same. The ring at the base fills as today's allowance and empties as the day goes on. It never shows a number and never refuses a puff.

105 mmby 22 by 12, 35 g. Pen format, flat oval section, matte anodised aluminium. One light ring, one haptic, zero buttons.
The evidence

Calibrated on the one public dataset of real vapers under a nicotine cut.

Twenty experienced vapers, one week each at 18 and 6 mg/ml, every puff logged by the device (Dawkins et al., 2018). It fixed the size of compensation, the craving response, and the fact that a single cut on its own does not make people relapse.

Zero

The last step is the same size as every other. Then there is nothing left to step down.

Deciding daily is not better than weekly unless the signals are faster than the decisions, and small steps are not free, withdrawal accumulates. Wane's job is the pace, and the pace is yours.

0 %nicotine share. The nicotine coil is off. The device keeps working, the flavour is the same.

Honest status

The engine is evaluated on synthetic people who obey the assumptions it was written with.

Every constant in the model is labelled by where it comes from. What remains synthetic until a pilot: night puffs, time to first puff, weekend patterns and months of tapering. The device is a concept sheet drawn to scale, not a render or a tested part.

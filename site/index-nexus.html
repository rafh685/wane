    04 — DOSE CONTROL

    THE DOSECOMES DOWN.

    Two coils and a power ratio, no mixer. A puff draws through both chambers; the app sets how much power each coil gets. Lower the nicotine coil's share week by week and the dose falls while the flavour stays the same.

    NICOTINE SHARE 02 MIDWAY

    20 % NICOTINE SHARE · AT 20 %, THE CHAMBER LASTS ABOUT 9 DAYS

    A COIL CANNOT VAPORISE MUCH BELOW 40 % POWER. THE LAST STRETCH IS SHORTER PUFFS, 4 S DOWN TO 1 S, THEN THE COIL SWITCHES OFF.

    BORING IS THE GOAL.

    Pen format, flat oval section so it does not roll. 35 g, heavier than a disposable on purpose: a JUUL is 95 × 15 × 8 mm and 15 g.

    Matte anodised aluminium, no candy colours, no logo on the front. The only feature on the front is the light ring. It sits next to a blood pressure monitor, not an Elf Bar.

    • 35G

    • 0BUTTONS

    01

    TWO
    CHAMBERS

    A wide flavour chamber, 1.4 ml, filled with any 0 mg liquid. A narrow nicotine chamber, 0.6 ml, filled from a standard 20 mg/ml nic shot. The two liquids never touch; only their vapour meets, in the mouthpiece.

    02

    LEVEL
    WINDOWS

    Each chamber has its own window. At a 20 % blend the nicotine chamber lasts about nine days; as the taper progresses it lasts longer. The one visible sign of progress on the hardware.

    03

    LIGHT
    RING

    One ring on the front, today's allowance as a fraction of a circle. Nothing else on the body: no numbers, no buttons, draw-activated. Friction lives in the app, not the hardware.
